## 🚨 Critical Issues Identified

### 1. Widespread Keyword Cannibalization (Critical)
GSC is routing traffic to the *wrong* pages for 3 out of 4 ranking keywords:

| Keyword | Designated Page | Page Google Chose |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| best ips | /what-makes-ips-best-bangladesh/ | /best-ips-brand-bangladesh/ |
| best ips brand in bangladesh | /best-ips-in-bangladesh/ | /best-ips-brand-bangladesh/ |
| lithium ips | /best-lithium-ips-bangladesh/ | /best-lithium-battery-for-ips-in-bangladesh/ |
| lithium battery | /best-lithium-battery-for-ips-in-bangladesh/ | / (homepage!) |

**Root cause:** Multiple pages with overlapping topical coverage. The content cluster blog posts (Top 10 IPS Brands, IPS Price Comparison, etc.) are cannibalizing the designated pillar pages.

**Action:** Consolidate or differentiate. For "best ips" cluster — ensure `/best-ips-in-bangladesh/` is significantly more comprehensive, then add canonical tags or internal link consolidation to funnel authority.

### 2. Bengali Keywords Need More Content Depth
- The `/sher-aips-company-bangladesh/` page is triggering adjacent Bengali queries (who makes the best IPS?) but not the exact target phrase.
- `/50-hazar-takar-ips-bangladesh/` has **zero** GSC data — likely not yet indexed or too new to accumulate impressions.

### 3. Homepage Cannibalizing "lithium battery"
- Google is ranking the homepage `/` for "lithium battery" at position 8.5, not the dedicated battery page. This dilutes the pillar page authority.
- Fix: Add more specific "lithium battery IPS" anchor text in internal links pointing to `/best-lithium-battery-for-ips-in-bangladesh/`.
