When a critically ill patient in Bangladesh needs specialist care that requires cross-border medical evacuation, Myanmar's proximity makes it a practical destination for air ambulance transfer. Our air ambulance service from Dhaka to Yangon or Mandalay provides a complete ICU-in-the-sky transfer, from the patient's bedside in Dhaka to a hospital bed in Myanmar without interruption of critical care.

The cost of an air ambulance from Dhaka to Yangon or Mandalay ranges from $25,000 to $32,000 USD (approximately BDT 29,00,000 to BDT 38,00,000). This is an all-inclusive charter price for a fully equipped flying ICU using aircraft such as a Learjet 35A, departing from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, Dhaka.

Why is it less expensive than Bangkok? The distance from Dhaka to Yangon is approximately 800 km and to Mandalay approximately 900 km — roughly half the distance to Bangkok (1,700 km). The shorter flight time (1.5–2 hours versus 3.5 hours) reduces fuel cost, crew time, and aircraft positioning costs. However, Myanmar hospitals have fewer JCI-accredited specialists and limited ICU capacity compared to Bumrungrad International Hospital or Samitivej Hospital in Bangkok. We recommend the best destination based on the patient's specific medical needs and required specialist availability.

Which Myanmar Hospitals Do We Transfer Patients To?

Myanmar's leading hospitals that regularly receive critically ill patients from Bangladesh via air ambulance include:

  • Pun Hlaing Siloam Hospital (Yangon) — Myanmar's only JCI-accredited hospital; strong in cardiac surgery, neurosurgery, oncology, and complex multi-organ care
  • Yangon General Hospital — Largest public teaching hospital; trauma, emergency medicine, and infectious disease referral centre
  • Mandalay General Hospital — Major referral centre for upper Myanmar; trauma and surgical specialities
  • Asia Royal Hospital (Yangon) — Private tertiary care; cardiology, neurology, and critical care

We maintain pre-arranged admission protocols and direct specialist contacts at these facilities. Before departure from Dhaka, we confirm the receiving ICU bed, specialist availability, and medical team readiness so the patient transitions seamlessly from aircraft to hospital bed.

Aircraft & Medical Crew

  • Aircraft: Learjet 35A (pressurised cabin, range 4,000+ km, cruise 830 km/h) — configured as a flying ICU
  • Flight time: Dhaka → Yangon ~1 hour 30 minutes; Dhaka → Mandalay ~1 hour 50 minutes
  • Medical crew: One flight doctor (emergency/critical care trained) + one flight paramedic per transfer
  • Equipment: Hamilton T1/MR1 transport ventilator, Zoll X Series cardiac monitor/defibrillator, Braun Perfusor Space infusion pumps, full ACLS drug kit, portable suction, 2× D-size oxygen cylinders

Bed-to-Bed Transfer Process

  1. Call 01716-960770 — 24/7 emergency line. We collect patient clinical details, current location, and destination preference.
  2. Clinical assessment — Our flight doctor reviews medical reports, speaks with the treating physician, and confirms flight fitness.
  3. Hospital coordination — We secure ICU bed confirmation and specialist acceptance at the receiving Myanmar hospital.
  4. Export clearance — We handle Bangladesh DGHS medical export permit and customs formalities.
  5. Ground logistics — Ambulance from sending hospital → Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport → aircraft → Yangon/Mandalay airport → receiving hospital.
  6. In-flight care — Continuous ventilator management, hemodynamic monitoring, medication titration, and real-time communication with receiving team.
  7. Handover — Structured clinical handover to receiving ICU team at hospital bedside.
