Air Ambulance Dhaka to Delhi India — ICU Jet 24/7

Fully equipped ICU jet transfers from Dhaka to Medanta Gurgaon, Fortis Escorts Delhi, and Max Hospital. Flight time 2.5 to 3 hours. Bed-to-bed service with flight doctor. Call 01716-960770.

Quick Answer

Air ambulance from Dhaka to Delhi costs between USD 22,000 and USD 30,000 for a fully equipped ICU charter. The Learjet 35A or King Air 350 flies directly from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport to Indira Gandhi International Airport (Delhi) in 2.5 to 3 hours, with a flight doctor providing continuous ICU monitoring. Ground transfer to Medanta or Fortis takes an additional 30-60 minutes.

What Does an Air Ambulance from Dhaka to Delhi Cost?

The cost of an air ambulance from Dhaka to Delhi ranges from USD 22,000 to USD 30,000 for a fully equipped ICU charter. This all-inclusive price covers the aircraft (Learjet 35A or King Air 350), flight doctor, ICU nurse, ventilator and monitoring equipment, ground ambulances at both ends, fuel, and overflight permits. For stable patients who do not require ICU-level care, a commercial medical escort is available from USD 5,500 to USD 12,000.

Delhi is one of the more affordable Indian destinations from Dhaka, comparable in price to the Chennai corridor. For comparison, Dhaka to Bangkok air ambulance cost runs USD 27,000 to 33,000, while Dhaka to Singapore is USD 32,000 to 42,000.

Which Delhi Hospitals Accept Air Ambulance Patients from Bangladesh?

Delhi NCR has several world-class hospitals configured to receive international air ambulance patients. We maintain direct admission agreements with each:

  • Medanta — The Medicity, Gurgaon — The largest multi-super-speciality hospital in the Delhi region. Flagship destination for complex cardiac surgery, liver and kidney transplant, neurosurgery, and oncology. Approximately 45 minutes from Delhi airport.
  • Fortis Escorts Heart Institute, Delhi — India's premier cardiac institute, pioneered interventional cardiology. Accepts emergency angioplasty and CABG referrals from across South Asia. Roughly 30 minutes from the airport.
  • Max Super Speciality Hospital, Saket — Strong in cardiac surgery, neurosurgery, oncology, and organ transplant. Dedicated international patient services desk with Bangladeshi patient experience.
  • Apollo Hospital, Sarita Vihar — Full multi-specialty campus with strong emergency and critical care services.

How Long Does the Flight from Dhaka to Delhi Take?

The direct flight from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) to Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) takes approximately 2.5 to 3 hours. This makes Delhi one of the shortest international flights from Bangladesh, which is clinically important for time-sensitive conditions like acute cardiac events or stroke.

Total door-to-bed time including ground transfers: approximately 5 to 7 hours for an emergency case. Ground ambulance from Delhi airport to Medanta Gurgaon adds 30 to 60 minutes depending on traffic.

What Is the Air Ambulance Process from Dhaka to Delhi?

  1. Call 01716-960770 — Our duty doctor reviews the patient's condition and recommends Delhi or an alternative destination.
  2. Aircraft mobilisation — ICU-configured jet dispatched from standby position. Positioning to Dhaka takes 1–2 hours.
  3. Medical visa coordination — We coordinate with the Indian High Commission for fast-track medical visa processing. Runs in parallel with aircraft mobilisation.
  4. Receiving hospital confirmation — Bed availability and specialist briefing confirmed at Medanta, Fortis, or Max before patient departure.
  5. Ground ambulance to Hazrat Shahjalal Airport — ICU ground ambulance with flight medical team.
  6. Flight (2.5–3 hours) — Continuous ICU monitoring, ventilator support as needed, and direct communication with receiving hospital.
  7. Arrival Delhi IGI Airport + handover — Ground ambulance meets flight on tarmac. Direct transfer to receiving hospital. Medical handover to specialist team.

Do I Need an Indian Medical Visa?

Yes — Bangladeshi citizens require a Medical Visa (M-Visa) for treatment in India. For genuine medical emergencies, the Indian High Commission in Dhaka issues fast-track medical visas within 24 to 48 hours when accompanied by a hospital invitation letter from the receiving Indian facility. For unconscious or intubated patients, the visa is issued on the basis of medical documentation without the patient attending in person. Family members travel on MX (medical attendant) visas. Our team assists with all visa documentation.

Is a Ventilated Patient Safe on the Dhaka to Delhi Flight?

Yes. The 2.5 to 3 hour flight is actually shorter than the Dhaka to Bangkok corridor, making it one of the safest international routes for critically ill patients. Our ICU air ambulances carry transport ventilators, cardiac monitors, infusion pumps, and defibrillators. The flight doctor manages the patient continuously. The cabin is pressurised to sea-level equivalent, eliminating the altitude-related risks of commercial flights.

Preparing for the Flight: Document Checklist

Preparing for a medical air transfer to Delhi requires careful document preparation. The following items should be gathered before the flight to avoid delays:

  • Valid passport — Patient and medical attendant must hold passports valid for at least six months beyond the planned travel date.
  • Indian Medical Visa (M-Visa) — Bangladeshi citizens require this visa for treatment in India. The Indian High Commission in Dhaka fast-tracks medical visas within 24 to 48 hours when accompanied by a hospital invitation letter.
  • MX Visa for attendant — Family members or caregivers travelling with the patient need a separate Medical Attendant visa.
  • Hospital invitation letter — From Medanta, Fortis Escorts, or Max Hospital confirming bed allocation and treating specialist. We coordinate this directly before departure.
  • Complete medical records — Discharge summary, imaging studies (CT, MRI, X-ray on CD), blood work, ECG, echocardiography, and surgical reports. Digital copies shared with the receiving hospital in advance to speed up admission.
  • Insurance and financial documents — Travel and medical insurance policies, pre-authorisation letters, and any embassy or corporate sponsorship letters.
  • Bangladesh DGHS export clearance — Handled entirely by our team. We manage the Directorate General of Health Services export permit and customs formalities.

Our coordination team guides families through each document requirement step by step. For a complete overview of the India transfer process, see our Dhaka to India air ambulance guide.

What Happens After Landing: Hospital Admission in Delhi NCR

Upon landing at Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), the pre-positioned ground ambulance meets the aircraft directly on the tarmac. The receiving hospital's international patient desk has already been briefed on the patient's clinical status, in-flight interventions, and treatment plan.

Medanta The Medicity, Gurgaon — The ground transfer takes 30 to 60 minutes depending on Delhi traffic. Medanta's international coordination team prepares the ICU bed (cardiac, neurological, or general) and the admitting specialist meets the patient at the ward for formal handover. All in-flight vitals and clinical changes are documented and communicated in real time.

Fortis Escorts Heart Institute — Located roughly 30 minutes from the airport. For cardiac emergencies including acute MI and post-CABG transfers, the catheterisation lab and cardiac ICU are on standby. Our flight doctor provides a structured handover to the interventional cardiologist or cardiac surgeon.

For patients requiring ICU-level care, the transition from aircraft to hospital bed is designed to be seamless. The equipment used in our Learjet 35A and King Air 350 matches standard ICU configurations, so there is no gap in monitoring during transfer. For cost details, see our air ambulance cost guide.
