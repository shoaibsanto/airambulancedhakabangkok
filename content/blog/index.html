Air Ambulance Service Dhaka to Bangkok: The Complete Guide

Everything families and referring doctors need to know about moving a critically ill patient safely from Dhaka to Bangkok — explained in plain language by our medical coordination team.

In Short

An air ambulance from Dhaka to Bangkok is a 24/7 flying ICU with doctor, nurse and bed-to-bed transfer in ~2.5–3 hours. Free assessment — call 01716-960770.

When a Bangladeshi patient needs treatment abroad, the journey itself is part of their care. A Dhaka-to-Bangkok air ambulance is a continuous chain of ICU monitoring from bedside to bedside. Explore our air ambulance services to see what is included on board.

Cost is almost always the first question families ask, and it deserves a clear, honest answer rather than a vague estimate. Pricing depends on the aircraft type, the level of medical equipment required, and whether the patient needs a dedicated ICU configuration or a medical escort on a commercial flight. Our breakdown of what a transfer costs walks through each factor so there are no surprises, while the transfer process explains how a transfer is organised from the first phone call to the final hospital handover.

Geography and timing also shape every decision. Bangkok is a leading regional destination for cardiac, neurological and oncology care, and the short flight time makes it a practical choice for unstable patients. To see which cities and corridors we operate across — and how quickly a team can be mobilised — review routes and coverage. If you would rather speak to a coordinator straight away, you can contact our 24/7 desk at any hour.
