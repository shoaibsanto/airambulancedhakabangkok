Air Ambulance Dhaka to Chennai India — ICU Jet 24/7

Fully equipped ICU jet transfers from Dhaka to Apollo Hospital Chennai, CMC Vellore, and other major Tamil Nadu medical centres. Flight time 3 to 3.5 hours. Call 01716-960770.

Quick Answer

Air ambulance from Dhaka to Chennai costs between USD 22,000 and USD 28,000 for a fully equipped ICU charter. The Learjet 35A or King Air 350 flies directly from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport to Chennai International Airport in 3 to 3.5 hours. For patients heading to CMC Vellore, a 2-hour ground transfer from Chennai completes the bed-to-bed journey.

What Does an Air Ambulance from Dhaka to Chennai Cost?

The cost of an air ambulance from Dhaka to Chennai ranges from USD 22,000 to USD 28,000 for a fully equipped ICU charter. This all-inclusive price covers the aircraft, flight doctor, ICU nurse, ventilator and monitoring equipment, ground ambulances at both ends, fuel, and overflight permits.

If the final destination is CMC Vellore (2 hours south of Chennai by road), the ground transfer is included in our bed-to-bed service. For stable patients, a commercial medical escort is available from USD 5,500 to USD 12,000.

Which Hospitals in Chennai Accept Air Ambulance Patients?

  • Apollo Hospitals, Chennai (Greams Road) — One of Asia's highest-volume cardiac surgery centres. Roughly 30 minutes from Chennai International Airport. 24/7 international patient services desk that pre-arranges bed allocation and direct ambulance reception. Strong in cardiac surgery, neurology, oncology, transplant, and trauma.
  • Christian Medical College (CMC), Vellore — The historic gold standard for South Asian patients. 2-hour ground transfer from Chennai airport. Renowned for paediatric cardiology, neurology, oncology, complex internal medicine, and organ transplant. Dedicated international office accepts pre-confirmed admissions from air ambulance services.
  • MIOT International Hospital, Chennai — Specialist orthopaedic and multi-organ transplant centre. 20 minutes from airport.
  • Kauvery Hospital, Chennai — Strong in cardiac surgery, neurosciences, and emergency medicine.

How Long Is the Flight from Dhaka to Chennai?

The direct flight from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC) to Chennai International Airport (MAA) takes approximately 3 to 3.5 hours. This is one of the most direct cross-border routes from Bangladesh to India.

  • Apollo Hospital Chennai — 30 minutes ground transfer from airport. Total door-to-bed: approximately 5 to 6 hours.
  • CMC Vellore — 2 hours ground transfer from Chennai airport. Total door-to-bed: approximately 6 to 8 hours.

What Medical Conditions Do We Transfer to Chennai?

  • Cardiac surgery — CABG, valve replacement, interventional cardiology (Apollo Chennai)
  • Paediatric cardiology — Congenital heart defects, neonatal cardiac surgery (CMC Vellore)
  • Oncology — Surgical oncology, bone marrow transplant, rare tumour treatment
  • Neurology and neurosurgery — Stroke, brain tumour, epilepsy surgery (CMC, Apollo)
  • Organ transplant — Liver, kidney, heart transplant evaluation and surgery
  • Trauma — Major polytrauma, industrial injuries, complex fractures

What Is the Air Ambulance Process from Dhaka to Chennai?

  1. Call 01716-960770 — Our duty doctor reviews the patient's condition and confirms Chennai as the optimal destination.
  2. Aircraft and visa mobilisation — ICU jet dispatched and medical visa coordination with Indian High Commission run in parallel.
  3. Hospital confirmation — Bed availability and specialist briefing confirmed at Apollo, CMC, or other receiving hospital.
  4. Ground ambulance to airport — ICU ground ambulance collects patient from ward in Dhaka.
  5. Flight (3–3.5 hours) — Continuous ICU monitoring, ventilator support, and communication with receiving hospital.
  6. Ground ambulance in Chennai — Meets flight on tarmac. Direct transfer to Apollo Chennai (30 min) or onward to CMC Vellore (2 hours).
  7. Medical handover — Formal clinical handover to the receiving specialist team at the destination hospital.

How Does CMC Vellore Transfer Work from Dhaka?

CMC Vellore is one of the most requested Bangladeshi patient destinations in India, but the hospital does not have its own airport. The standard route is:

  1. ICU air ambulance from Dhaka (DAC) directly to Chennai (MAA) — 3 to 3.5 hours flight
  2. Ground ambulance from Chennai airport to CMC Vellore — 2 hours by road
  3. Total bed-to-bed time: approximately 6 to 8 hours

We manage the full chain: both ground ambulances, airport coordination in Chennai, and pre-confirmed admission at CMC's international office. For the complete overview, see our Dhaka to India air ambulance guide.

Do I Need an Indian Medical Visa for Chennai?

Yes — Bangladeshi citizens require a Medical Visa (M-Visa). In genuine emergencies, the Indian High Commission in Dhaka issues fast-track medical visas within 24 to 48 hours with a hospital invitation letter. For unconscious patients, the visa is issued on medical documentation alone. Family members travel on MX visas. Our team assists with all documentation.
