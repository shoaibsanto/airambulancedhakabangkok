Medical Evacuation Cost from Bangladesh — Complete 2026 Guide

Every route, every service level, every cost component — the definitive pricing guide for medical evacuation from Bangladesh in 2026. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Quick Answer

Medical evacuation from Bangladesh in 2026 ranges from USD 3,000 to USD 42,000 depending on destination and service level. The most common routes: Dhaka to Bangkok ICU jet costs $27,000–$33,000; Dhaka to India (Delhi/Chennai) costs $22,000–$30,000; Dhaka to Singapore costs $32,000–$42,000. Commercial medical escort is available from $3,000 to $14,000 for stable patients.

What Are the Individual Cost Components?

Breaking the total into components helps families understand what they are paying for and compare quotes from different operators.

1. Aircraft Charter Fee (60–70% of total)

The largest single component. Covers the aircraft, pilots, fuel, landing and navigation fees. On international routes, the operator also factors in positioning — the aircraft may fly empty to Dhaka from a regional base. Expect the aircraft line alone to run USD 8,000–28,000 depending on jet category and distance.

2. Medical Crew (15–20% of total)

A standard ICU crew includes a flight doctor, critical-care nurse, and paramedic. Crew costs are either bundled or billed separately at USD 1,500–3,500 per crew member. A three-person ICU team adds USD 3,000–6,000 to the total.

3. Medical Equipment and Consumables (5–8% of total)

Transport ventilators, cardiac monitors, defibrillators, infusion pumps, medications, and airway kits. Budget USD 800–2,500. Specialist items like incubators or intra-aortic balloon pumps carry additional charges.

4. Ground Ambulances, Both Ends (3–5% of total)

ALS ground ambulances collect the patient from the Dhaka hospital and deliver them to the aircraft, and a receiving ambulance meets the flight at the destination. Both legs together add USD 600–1,500.

5. Flight Permits and Handling (2–4% of total)

International air ambulance flights require overflight and landing permits from civil aviation authorities. Handling agents coordinate fuel, parking, and customs. This adds USD 400–1,000 and is included in reputable operators' all-in quotes.

Why Does Bangkok Cost More Than Delhi or Chennai?

While Bangkok is geographically closer to Dhaka than Delhi, the total cost is higher for several reasons: Thailand's aviation handling fees and overflight permits are higher, the receiving hospital (Bumrungrad) charges premium international patient rates, and the total logistics chain for the Bangkok corridor is more complex. For patients where both destinations offer equivalent specialist care, India can save 15–25% on the total transfer cost.

However, Bangkok's Bumrungrad International Hospital offers world-class infrastructure and may be the better clinical choice for cardiac emergencies, stroke, and post-surgical care. The decision should always be medically driven — cost is one factor, not the only factor.

How Does Insurance Cover Medical Evacuation from Bangladesh?

Many international travel health insurance and employer-sponsored group policies include medical evacuation benefits. Key points:

  • Pre-authorisation is essential — Contact your insurer before the flight departs. We provide the documentation they need.
  • Direct billing is possible — Some insurers pay the operator directly, eliminating out-of-pocket costs.
  • Reimbursement is standard — For families who pay upfront, we provide itemised invoices for insurance claims.
  • Bumrungrad has insurance partnerships — The hospital works with several international insurance networks for direct billing.

For detailed guidance, see our insurance coverage guide and insurance payment from Dhaka to Bumrungrad.

Not automatically. But a quote that is 20% cheaper because it uses a basic nurse escort instead of a doctor-led ICU crew is not the same service. Always verify: Is the crew certified? Is the aircraft equipped to the patient's clinical needs? Does the operator carry liability insurance? Can they provide references from recent missions?
