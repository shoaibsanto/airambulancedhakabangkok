Air Ambulance Service Dhaka to Bangkok

End-to-end ICU air ambulance, medical evacuation and commercial medical escort services moving critically ill and injured patients safely from Dhaka, Bangladesh to leading Bangkok hospitals — fully monitored, doctor-led and available 24/7.

What We Provide

What Complete Medical Flight Solutions Do We Offer?

Every transfer is a single coordinated chain of care — from the patient's bedside in Dhaka to admission at a hospital in Bangkok. Our team handles the air ambulance, the flight doctor and paramedic, the medical equipment, the ground ambulances on both ends, and the documentation, so families can focus on their loved one rather than logistics.

1. ICU Air Ambulance (Charter Jet)

A dedicated air ambulance charter jet configured as a flying intensive care unit. The aircraft carries a transport ventilator, multi-parameter monitor, infusion pumps and oxygen reserves, staffed by a critical care flight doctor and paramedic. Ideal for unstable patients, ventilator-dependent cases and time-critical medical evacuation where a non-stop, controlled environment is essential between Dhaka and Bangkok.

2. Commercial Airline Medical Escort

For stable patients who do not need a charter jet, we arrange a commercial medical escort on scheduled flights. A trained flight doctor or nurse travels alongside the patient with portable oxygen, medications and monitoring, plus pre-arranged stretcher or extra-legroom seating. This is a cost-effective alternative for routine repatriation and recovery transfers to Bangkok.

3. Ground Ambulance & Bed-to-Bed Transfer

True bed-to-bed transfer means the patient never has to be handed off into uncertainty. We dispatch a fully equipped ground ambulance to lift the patient from their hospital bed or home in Dhaka, transport them to the aircraft, and a second ambulance receives them in Bangkok for the final leg to the admitting hospital — one continuous, monitored journey under our care.

4. Medical Repatriation & Return

When a patient stabilises after treatment in Bangkok, we manage their safe return to Dhaka or onward to home. Our repatriation service covers post-operative patients, accident survivors and chronic cases, coordinating discharge summaries, fit-to-fly clearance and continuity of care so the homecoming is just as carefully supervised as the outbound flight.

5. Neonatal, Paediatric & Cardiac Critical Transfer

Specialised transfers for the most fragile patients. Our neonatal and paediatric service uses a transport incubator and dedicated specialists for newborns and children, while cardiac critical care transfers carry advanced monitoring, defibrillation and IABP-ready protocols. These complex cases are escorted by experienced critical care teams trained for high-acuity medical evacuation.

6. Visa, Permits & Documentation Assistance

Cross-border medical evacuation depends on paperwork being right the first time. We assist with Thai medical visas, hospital invitation letters, landing and overflight permits, customs clearance for medical equipment, and insurance liaison. Our coordinators work directly with Bangkok hospitals including Bumrungrad International Hospital to confirm admission before departure.

On-Board Capability

What Medical Equipment Is Available on Every Flight?

Our ICU air ambulance is equipped to the standard of a hospital intensive care unit, allowing the flight doctor and paramedic to deliver uninterrupted critical care at altitude. The same continuity of monitoring extends through every ground ambulance leg of the route to Bangkok, so no patient is ever moved without full clinical oversight.

Curious how a transfer unfolds from your first call to hospital admission? Walk through it on our step-by-step process page.

  • Transport ventilator with adjustable modes for adults, children and neonates
  • Multi-parameter monitor: ECG, SpO₂, NIBP, capnography and temperature
  • Defibrillator with external pacing for cardiac critical care
  • Infusion and syringe pumps for precise drug and fluid delivery
  • Medical-grade oxygen with calculated reserves for the full route
  • Portable suction, intubation kit and a comprehensive emergency drug box
  • Transport incubator available for neonatal and paediatric transfers
All-Inclusive Care

What's Included in Your Air Ambulance Service?

We quote complete, transparent packages so there are no surprises during an already stressful time. A standard Dhaka to Bangkok medical evacuation includes everything needed to move the patient from one hospital bed to another.

Dedicated Medical Team

A critical care flight doctor and paramedic or nurse accompany every patient, with team composition matched to the patient's acuity. They manage the ventilator, medications and monitoring continuously and hand over a full clinical report to the receiving Bangkok hospital.

Door-to-Door Coordination

Ground ambulances at both ends, ramp access, airport coordination and a single point of contact who keeps the family updated throughout. You speak to one team, not five vendors.

Permits & Documentation

Flight clearances, overflight and landing permits, medical visas, fit-to-fly assessment and insurance correspondence are all handled in advance so departure is never delayed by paperwork.
