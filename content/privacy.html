Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026. This policy explains how Air Ambulance Dhaka to Bangkok collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you contact us about an air ambulance transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok, request a medical assessment, or use our website.

1. Who We Are

Who Is Responsible for Your Information?

Air Ambulance Dhaka to Bangkok is the data controller for the personal information collected through this website and through direct contact with our 24/7 medical coordination desk. Our operating address is Ground Floor, Surecell Medical BD Ltd, Plot 2 Rd 21, Dhaka 1212, Bangladesh. You can reach our privacy team at info@airambulancedhakabangkok.com or by calling 01716-960770 any time, day or night.

2. Information We Collect

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

To arrange an air ambulance transfer we need medical and logistical information about the patient and the people contacting us. The categories we collect include:

  • Patient details: name, age, weight, current hospital, diagnosis, current condition, treating physician, required medical equipment and a contact for the receiving hospital.
  • Contact details: name, phone number, email, WhatsApp number and relationship to the patient for the family member or case manager who first reaches us.
  • Case communication: medical reports, discharge summaries, fit-to-fly certificates and any other documents you choose to share with our coordination team.
  • Payment and billing information: invoicing party, payment method, and the records we are required to keep for financial compliance.
  • Website analytics: anonymised IP, device type, pages visited and referring source, used only to understand how the site is used and to improve it.
3. How We Use Your Information

How Do We Use the Information You Share?

We use the information you share only to provide the air ambulance service you have asked for and to operate the website safely. Specifically:

  • To assess the patient's clinical suitability for air transfer and recommend the safest option.
  • To coordinate with air operators, hospitals, ground ambulance providers, embassies and immigration authorities on your behalf.
  • To provide written quotations and to invoice for the agreed services.
  • To comply with medical record keeping, aviation and financial regulations in Bangladesh and Thailand.
  • To respond to your direct enquiries and improve our website.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers or marketing networks.

4. Legal Basis

What Is the Legal Basis for Processing?

We process your information on the basis of contractual necessity (to deliver the air ambulance service you request), explicit consent for sensitive medical data, legal obligation for the records we are required to keep, and legitimate interest in operating a safe and reliable service.

5. How We Share Information

Who Else Receives Your Information?

To deliver a transfer we share the minimum information required with: the air operator and flight medical crew, the receiving hospital in Bangkok, ground ambulance partners at both ends, immigration and customs authorities in Bangladesh and Thailand, and our auditors or legal advisers where required. All third parties are bound by confidentiality and operate only under our instructions.

6. Storage and Security

How Do We Store and Protect Your Data?

Patient records, contact information and case notes are stored on encrypted systems with strict access controls. Only the coordination team members directly involved in your case can see your information. Paper records are kept in locked storage at our Dhaka office and are digitised and archived within seven days of creation.

7. Retention

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

We retain case records for at least seven years to satisfy medical record and aviation audit requirements. After that period, records are securely destroyed unless we have a legal reason to retain them longer. You may request earlier deletion of records that are not subject to a retention obligation.

8. Your Rights

What Rights Do You Have Over Your Data?

You have the right to access, correct, port, restrict and request deletion of your personal data, and to object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest. To exercise any of these rights, write to info@airambulancedhakabangkok.com. We respond within thirty days.

9. Cookies

What Cookies and Tracking Technologies Do We Use?

We use a minimal set of cookies: one to remember whether you have dismissed the call bar notification, and one for anonymised analytics that helps us understand which pages are useful. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not embed third-party tracking pixels.

10. International Transfers

Do We Transfer Data Outside Bangladesh?

Yes. Air ambulance transfers to Bangkok require sharing the patient's medical and travel information with Thai hospitals, Thai immigration and the receiving flight crew. We share only what is necessary and rely on contractual safeguards to keep that information protected.

11. Children and Minors

How Do We Handle Data About Minors?

Our service regularly transfers critically ill children and neonates. Information about minors is collected only from parents or legal guardians and is processed under the same safeguards as adult patient data.

12. Changes to This Policy

How Will We Notify You of Changes?

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for at least thirty days and the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

13. Contact

How Can You Reach Our Privacy Team?

Write to info@airambulancedhakabangkok.com or call our 24/7 desk at 01716-960770. Postal correspondence can be addressed to the Privacy Officer, Ground Floor, Surecell Medical BD Ltd, Plot 2 Rd 21, Dhaka 1212, Bangladesh.
