Stroke & Neurology Emergency Evacuation Dhaka to Bangkok

A fast ICU air ambulance for stroke, brain haemorrhage, spinal cord injury and other neuro-critical patients. We fly from Dhaka to Bumrungrad International Hospital Bangkok. The flight is doctor-led, fully monitored and ready 24/7.

Quick Answer

Stroke & Neurology Evacuation Dhaka to Bangkok — At a Glance

What is a stroke and neurology emergency evacuation? It is a dedicated ICU air ambulance that moves stroke and neuro-critical patients from Dhaka to Bangkok. The patient stays under specialist care the whole way. The aircraft carries a neuro-capable ventilator, a multi-parameter monitor, ICP monitoring, and a full neurological emergency drug box. A flight doctor trained in neuro-critical care is on board. Costs depend on the aircraft, crew and equipment — see our air ambulance cost guide. Patients are treated at Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok. This service requires the full ICU air ambulance package and takes about 3–5 hours from start to finish through our coordinated transfer process. For cardiac emergencies, see our dedicated cardiac cardiac emergency transfer service.

Neurological Emergency Transfer

Why Does a Dedicated Neuro Air Ambulance Matter for Stroke Patients?

In stroke and neurological emergencies, every minute counts. A patient may have an ischaemic stroke, a haemorrhagic stroke, a traumatic brain injury, spinal cord compression or status epilepticus. Recovery or permanent disability often comes down to two things. How fast they reach a full neuroscience centre, and the care they get on the way. For more on stroke air ambulance transfers from Dhaka to Bumrungrad — including neurological ICU equipment, a stroke specialist escort, and fast response — see our stroke air ambulance guide.

Neuro-Protective Ventilation & Monitoring

Our transport ventilator supports neuro-protective ventilation. It holds precise PaCO₂ targets to help avoid further brain injury. The multi-parameter monitor tracks end-tidal CO₂, SpO₂, ECG and invasive blood pressure without a break. For some cases we can carry intracranial pressure (ICP) monitoring equipment. This lets the flight doctor manage cerebral perfusion pressure through the whole transfer.

Stroke-Specific Medications on Board

Our drug box includes thrombolytics, anticonvulsants, mannitol, antihypertensives, sedation and reversal agents. This covers what is needed to manage a worsening neurological patient at altitude. The flight doctor can give tissue plasminogen activator (tPA) in flight if the therapeutic window allows. They can also manage raised intracranial pressure until the patient reaches Bumrungrad's neurosurgical team.

Direct Admission to Bumrungrad Neuroscience Centre

We plan every neurological transfer with Bumrungrad International Hospital's Neuroscience Centre in Bangkok. We alert their 24/7 stroke team, interventional neuroradiologists, neurosurgeons and neuro-ICU before we take off. So CT/MRI imaging, thrombectomy or surgery can start the moment the patient arrives.

Bed-to-Bed Neuro-Critical Transfer

From the ICU bed in Dhaka to the neuro-ICU at Bumrungrad, the patient stays under constant neurological monitoring. Our team handles every step — ground ambulance pickup, rampside transfer, the ICU flight and the final ground leg — with no handoff gaps. The flight doctor gives a full clinical handover to the neurologist who takes over.

People Also Ask

Common Questions About Stroke & Neurology Air Ambulance Transfers

What is a stroke emergency air ambulance from Dhaka to Bangkok?

A stroke emergency air ambulance is a dedicated ICU charter jet set up to care for neurological patients during transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok. The cabin has neuro-protective ventilation, constant multi-parameter monitoring, ICP monitoring and a full neurological drug box. The medical team includes a flight doctor trained in neuro-critical care. This service is built for patients with acute ischaemic or haemorrhagic stroke, traumatic brain injury, spinal cord injury, brain tumours, status epilepticus and other neurological emergencies that need urgent care at Bumrungrad International Hospital.

How much does a neurology evacuation cost from Dhaka to Bangkok?

Cost depends on the aircraft type, the crew, the equipment needed (such as ICP monitoring), and how urgent the case is. We give clear, itemised quotes after we review the patient's neurological reports. Visit our air ambulance cost page for a full breakdown of what drives the price of neuro-critical transfers.

Which Bangkok hospital treats neurology emergency patients transferred by air ambulance?

We take all our neurology emergency patients to Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok. It has a dedicated Neuroscience Centre with 24/7 stroke care, interventional neuroradiology, neurosurgery, and neuro-intensive care. Learn more on our Bangkok hospitals page.

What neurological equipment is on board the air ambulance?

Our neurology air ambulance includes: transport ventilator with neuro-protective modes, 5-lead ECG/ST-segment monitor, capnography, invasive and non-invasive blood pressure monitoring, ICP monitoring (on request), infusion/syringe pumps, blood gas analyser capability, and a comprehensive neurological emergency drug box including thrombolytics, anticonvulsants, mannitol and sedation.

What services are included in the stroke evacuation package?

The full package includes: a ground ambulance in Dhaka and Bangkok, a dedicated charter aircraft, a flight doctor and critical-care paramedic with neuro experience, all neurological monitoring and life-support equipment, permits and landing clearances, medical visa support, and direct coordination with Bumrungrad's neuroscience team. See our complete services page for more.

How does the stroke evacuation process work step by step?

Step 1: Call our 24/7 desk. Step 2: Neurological assessment and aircraft recommendation. Step 3: Coordinate admission with Bumrungrad Neuroscience Centre. Step 4: Ground ambulance collects patient in Dhaka. Step 5: Bedside-to-ramp transfer, depart under neuro-critical monitoring. Step 6: Fly to Bangkok with continuous care. Step 7: Ground ambulance to Bumrungrad neuro-ICU. See our transfer process page for the full step-by-step breakdown.
