About Our Air Ambulance Service Dhaka to Bangkok

We are a dedicated, Dhaka-based aeromedical coordination team built around a single promise — to move critically ill and injured patients from Dhaka to Bangkok safely, swiftly, and with continuous medical care from bed to bed.

Who We Are

Why Choose a Trusted Name for Dhaka-to-Bangkok Medical Transfers?

Air Ambulance Dhaka to Bangkok is a specialist air ambulance service and medical escort coordination team operating from the Ground Floor of Surecell Medical BD Ltd, Plot 2 Rd 21, Dhaka 1212. We exist for one purpose: to give families a calm, capable point of contact when a loved one needs urgent, life-supporting transfer from Bangladesh to a tertiary hospital in Thailand.

When a patient cannot travel on a normal commercial flight — because they require an ICU bed in the air, a ventilator, infusion support, or constant physician monitoring — every hour matters and every detail must be handled by people who do this work every day. That is exactly what we do. From the first phone call to the moment the patient is handed over to the receiving ICU in Bangkok, our coordinators manage the medical assessment, aircraft selection, crew, ground ambulances, airport clearances, and hospital admission as one seamless chain of care.

We are not a broker that disappears after taking a deposit. We are a hands-on coordination service that stays on the line, in the loop, and on the tarmac until the transfer is complete.

Our Mission

How Does Patient-First Care Work on Every Transfer?

Our mission is to remove fear and friction from the most stressful moments a family can face — and replace them with clear answers, qualified medical care, and a transfer plan you can trust.

Care Before Commerce

Clinical safety and patient comfort drive every decision we make — aircraft, crew, equipment, and timing are chosen for the patient, never to cut corners.

Speed With Diligence

Family Partnership

Experience & Expertise

Who Are the People and Partners Behind Every Flight?

A safe Dhaka-to-Bangkok air ambulance transfer is the product of trained people working together. Our coordination model brings together aeromedical-qualified clinicians and vetted aviation partners under one accountable team.

Aeromedical Flight Doctors

Our network includes physicians trained in aeromedical and critical-care transport, experienced in managing cardiac, neurological, trauma, and post-surgical patients at altitude. We manage cancer oncology patient transfers to Bangkok and trauma accident and trauma evacuation services with the same comprehensive level of care.

Paramedics & ICU Nurses

Critical-care nurses and flight paramedics accompany the patient, managing ventilation, infusions, monitoring, and emergency interventions throughout the journey.

Licensed Air Operators

We coordinate with licensed, insured air ambulance and charter operators whose aircraft are configured and maintained for medical missions, not improvised transport.

Receiving-Hospital Liaison

We liaise directly with Bumrungrad International Hospital to confirm bed availability, share clinical handover details, and arrange a smooth ICU-to-ICU admission on arrival.

Our Commitment

How We Work Alongside Families

The hours surrounding a medical emergency are overwhelming. Our commitment is to carry the operational and clinical burden so families can focus on their loved one. From the first conversation, we listen carefully, gather the patient's current medical condition, and explain — in plain language — exactly what an air ambulance transfer to Bangkok will involve.

We confirm clinical fitness to fly, recommend the right aircraft and crew, prepare a transparent quotation, and only proceed once you are comfortable. Throughout the mission you receive updates at each milestone: departure readiness, take-off, in-flight status, landing, and the final hospital handover. Should the patient's needs change mid-journey, our medical crew is equipped and authorised to respond.

This combination of experience, clinical expertise, transparent process, and constant communication is why families across Bangladesh trust us with their most important transfers. Have questions before you decide? Visit our FAQ or contact our team any time, day or night.
