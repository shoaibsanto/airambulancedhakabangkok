Quick Answer

The charter price covers a fully equipped flying ICU: aircraft, medical crew, ventilator, monitors, oxygen, bed-to-bed ground ambulances and full coordination. What Factors Determine the Air Ambulance Cost from Dhaka to Bangkok?

Six main factors influence the price of a Dhaka–Bangkok medical transfer. None of them are hidden surcharges — they simply reflect what it actually takes to move a particular patient safely. The table below shows how each one tends to affect the total.

Cost factorHow it affects the price
Aircraft type — dedicated ICU charter jet vs commercial medical escortThe single biggest driver. A dedicated charter jet costs significantly more than a medical escort on a scheduled commercial flight, because you are reserving the whole aircraft and crew.
Patient's medical condition & equipmentHigher acuity raises the cost. A ventilated, unstable or critical patient needs more equipment (ventilator, monitors, pumps) and oxygen reserves than a stable, recovering patient.
Medical crewVariable. A larger or more specialised team — for example a critical care doctor plus paramedic, or neonatal/cardiac specialists — increases the cost compared with a single escorting nurse.
Distance & flight permitsLargely fixed for this route, but landing, overflight and clearance permits add to the total and depend on the aircraft and timing.
Ground ambulance & bed-to-bed transferAdds a modest, predictable amount. Equipped ambulances at both the Dhaka and Bangkok ends are included so the journey is continuous bed-to-bed.
Time of booking / urgencyVariable. A same-day emergency activation can cost more than a planned transfer arranged a few days ahead, due to aircraft and crew availability.

Because the aircraft choice has such a large impact, it is worth understanding how choosing between an ICU air ambulance charter jet requires a higher budget, while a commercial medical escort costs significantly less for stable patients. Your chosen Bangkok hospital also influences planning — whether you are treated at Bumrungrad International Hospital. Our team operates from Dhaka and coordinates with the hospital's international admissions desk. The full transfer process takes approximately 4–8 hours from initial call to hospital handover. If you have travel or health insurance, much of the transfer may be covered by your policy — we can verify this during your free assessment.

What Is Included in the Air Ambulance Price?

An all-inclusive quote means the figure you are given covers the complete bed-to-bed journey. A standard Dhaka to Bangkok transfer package includes:

  • The aircraft and flight crew for the full route
  • ICU equipment — transport ventilator, multi-parameter monitor and infusion pumps as required
  • A dedicated medical team matched to the patient's acuity
  • Ground ambulances at both ends for a true bed-to-bed transfer
  • Full coordination — permits, airport handling, hospital liaison and a single point of contact
  • Basic in-flight medication and medical-grade oxygen for the journey

How Does Payment, Insurance & Documentation Work for Air Ambulance?

We aim to make payment as straightforward as possible during what is already a difficult time. Most families pay by bank transfer or another agreed method, and terms are confirmed in writing before departure so there are no surprises. For urgent cases we work to keep the administrative steps short so they never delay the medical flight.

We regularly handle insurance, embassy and corporate-sponsored transfers. If the patient has travel or health insurance, we can liaise directly with the insurer and supply the medical justification they need. Embassy repatriations and company-sponsored cases are equally welcome, and we are used to working within the approval processes those bodies require.

For every transfer we provide the documentation needed to support a claim or reimbursement — including the medical report, fit-to-fly assessment, itemised invoice and proof of service. If you are unsure how the funding will work, walk through it with us alongside our transfer process and we will explain each step clearly.
