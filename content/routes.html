Air Ambulance Service Dhaka to Bangkok — Routes & Coverage

Our dedicated corridor runs from Dhaka to Bangkok for Bumrungrad International Hospital, with nationwide ground ambulance pickup across Bangladesh and full repatriation flights back home.

Primary Corridor

What Is Our Core ICU Flight Route from Dhaka to Bangkok?

The Dhaka to Bangkok corridor is the heart of our operation. A direct medical flight covers roughly 2.5 to 3 hours in the air, making Bangkok one of the fastest international destinations from Bangladesh for time-critical patient transfers. For someone in an unstable cardiac, neurological or trauma condition, those few hours can be the difference between a routine transfer and a crisis — which is why every leg is flown with continuous ICU-grade monitoring, oxygen, ventilator support and a qualified medical escort on board.

Thailand draws Bangladeshi patients because Bangkok's leading hospitals combine advanced sub-specialty care with international patient services. Bumrungrad International Hospital is renowned for complex cardiac surgery, interventional cardiology, cancer treatment, neurology and neurosurgery. Patients commonly travel for cancer cancer patient air ambulance transfers, cardiac surgery and stenting, oncology and chemotherapy, neurosurgery and stroke care, organ transplant evaluation, and major trauma rehabilitation. We coordinate the air ambulance leg so the patient arrives stable, on time, and directly into Bumrungrad.

Departure Coverage

Where Do We Fly From in Bangladesh?

Wherever the patient is, our medical evacuation team arranges the air ambulance departure plus ground ambulance pickup from any district — bed-to-bed, door to door.

DhakaHazrat Shahjalal Intl (DAC)

Our home base. Fastest mobilisation, with bed-to-bed transfers from any hospital in the capital straight to the aircraft.

RajshahiShah Makhdum (RJH)

Western-region patients are stabilised and transferred to the nearest suitable airport for the medical flight onward.

KhulnaJashore Intl (JSR)

South-western coverage via Jashore, with ground ambulance relay from Khulna and surrounding districts.

Cox's BazarCox's Bazar Airport (CXB)

Coastal and remote-region evacuation, including tourist and emergency cases requiring urgent transfer to Bangkok.

Not on this list? We still come to you. Our team arranges ground ambulance pickup from any district in Bangladesh and routes the patient to the most appropriate departure airport. See how a transfer is planned step by step on our process page.

Receiving Hospital

Where Do Patients Arrive in Bangkok?

Bumrungrad International Hospital

One of Asia's largest private hospitals and a JCI-accredited facility, known for complex cardiac surgery, interventional cardiology, oncology, neurology and transplant evaluation. We coordinate directly with Bumrungrad's international admissions team.

We liaise with Bumrungrad's international department before departure so a bed, specialist and admission are confirmed — turning the trip into a true bed-to-bed transfer rather than a drop-off at the terminal.

Return & Repatriation

How Do Bangkok to Dhaka Return Flights Work?

Coverage works both ways. Once treatment at Bumrungrad is complete, we fly repatriation flights from Bangkok back to Dhaka — bringing recovering patients home under continued medical supervision. Whether the patient still needs ICU support, oxygen and ventilation, or simply a qualified medical escort for a long journey, we match the aircraft and crew to the clinical situation.

Repatriation is fully coordinated: discharge summaries are collected, medication and equipment are arranged for the flight, immigration and customs are pre-cleared, and a ground ambulance waits in Dhaka to complete the bed-to-bed handover at the receiving hospital or home. Learn more about our repatriation and medical escort services.

  • ICU air ambulance service return with full life support
  • Commercial medical escort for stable patients
  • Stretcher and oxygen arranged on scheduled flights
  • Discharge paperwork and medication handover managed
  • Ground ambulance waiting on arrival in Dhaka
  • Bed-to-bed handover at hospital or home
Ground Network

How Does Nationwide Ground Ambulance Pickup Work?

An air ambulance is only one part of a safe transfer. The most dangerous moments often happen on the ground — moving a critical patient from the ward to the vehicle, and from the vehicle to the aircraft. That is why our service is built around bed-to-bed continuity: the same standard of monitoring and care follows the patient from the originating hospital bed all the way to Bumrungrad in Bangkok.

We provide ground ambulance pickup from any district in Bangladesh — Dhaka, Rajshahi, Khulna, Cox's Bazar and every town in between. Each road leg uses an equipped ICU or basic-life-support ambulance staffed by trained personnel, synchronised with the flight schedule so there is no waiting and no gap in care. On the Bangkok side, a receiving ground ambulance and Bumrungrad's international team complete the chain. Have a question about how it all connects? Our FAQ covers the most common ones.
