Quick answers

What Contact & Enquiry Questions Do Families Ask?

How quickly will my enquiry be answered?

Our emergency desk is staffed 24/7. Phone calls are answered immediately. WhatsApp messages typically receive a reply within 2-5 minutes during business hours and within 10 minutes overnight. Email enquiries are acknowledged within 30 minutes and receive a detailed response within 1-2 hours.

What information should I have ready when I call?

To help us respond faster, please have the patient's current hospital and location, the receiving hospital in Bangkok (if already confirmed), a brief summary of the medical condition and care needs (ventilator, cardiac monitoring, isolation), the number of accompanying family members, and the desired transfer date and time. If you do not have all these details, call anyway — our coordinators will guide you through each step.

Can I get a cost estimate over the phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. During your initial call or WhatsApp conversation, our team will ask about the patient's medical requirements, the number of passengers and the desired timing. Based on this, we provide a preliminary cost estimate within minutes. A detailed written quotation with aircraft options and a full breakdown is sent by email or WhatsApp within 1-2 hours. See our cost and pricing page for typical price ranges.

What languages do your coordinators speak?

Our medical flight coordinators speak English and Bengali fluently. For patients or family members who speak other languages, we can arrange an interpreter within minutes via conference call. Our Bangkok-based partners also provide Thai, Mandarin and Hindi language support at the receiving hospital.

Can a family member visit your Dhaka office in person?

Yes. Our coordination office is located at Surecell Medical BD Ltd, Ground Floor, Plot 2 Road 21, Dhaka 1212. We recommend calling ahead so a dedicated coordinator is available to meet you. Walk-in visitors are welcome during business hours, but for urgent transfers we always recommend calling the 24/7 hotline first, as the fastest response comes through our phone and WhatsApp channels.
