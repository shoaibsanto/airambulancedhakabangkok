Cancer Patient Air Ambulance Transfer Dhaka to Bangkok

Safe, monitored ICU air ambulance for cancer patients from Dhaka to Bumrungrad International Hospital Bangkok. We run doctor-led, bed-to-bed transfers for chemotherapy, radiation, surgery and palliative cancer care.

Quick Answer

What Does a Cancer Patient Transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok Involve?

What is a cancer patient air ambulance transfer? A dedicated ICU air ambulance or commercial medical escort service that moves cancer patients from Dhaka to Bangkok under constant medical care. We tailor each transfer to the patient's diagnosis. Some patients need oxygen therapy, pain management or IV medications. Others need full ICU support during the flight. Costs depend on aircraft type, medical crew and equipment. See our air ambulance cost guide for details. Patients are treated at Bumrungrad International Hospital's Cancer Centre in Bangkok. This service requires a medical escort or ICU air ambulance support. It takes about 3–5 hours end-to-end through our coordinated transfer process.

Oncology Air Transfer

Why Transfer a Cancer Patient to Bangkok?

More Bangladeshi patients now travel to Bangkok for advanced cancer care. There they can reach new therapies, clinical trials, precision medicine and full team-based cancer care that may not be on offer at home. But many cancer patients are too sick, immunocompromised or post-surgical to take a normal commercial flight. An air ambulance or medical escort bridges the gap between their hospital in Bangladesh and the specialist oncology centre in Thailand.

Dedicated Oncology Medical Team

Our flight doctors and nurses know how to care for cancer patients. They handle symptom control, pain management and transfusion support. They also watch for problems such as febrile neutropenia, superior vena cava obstruction or malignant effusions. We match the team to each patient's cancer type and current condition.

Oncology Air Ambulance: Dhaka to Bangkok Cost, Aircraft & Admission Flow

For Bangladeshi oncology patients, the main value of a dedicated air ambulance is continuity of critical medication, blood-product storage, and a medical flight team that understands cancer-transfer protocols. The flight from Dhaka to Bangkok is typically arranged from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport and takes about 3.5 hours aboard a Learjet 35A or King Air 350 configured with ventilator capacity, infusion pumps, and portable oxygen. Charter prices for this route usually fall between USD 27,000 and USD 33,000 — roughly BDT 32,00,000 to BDT 39,00,000 using Indian-subcontinent comma formatting.

Once in Bangkok, handover to the receiving oncology unit is bed-to-bed: the flight doctor briefs the Bumrungrad or Bangkok Hospital cancer team, imaging and biopsy slides are passed directly to pathology, and admission paperwork is completed in the International Patient Centre. Many Bangladeshi patients already hold a Thai medical visa invitation letter before departure; if not, the hospital issues it within hours so that family members can accompany the patient.

Typical admissions covered by this corridor include targeted-therapy workups for breast, lung, and gastrointestinal cancers; haematology transfers for leukaemia or lymphoma requiring specialised chemotherapy protocols; and post-surgical reconstructive cases that need ICU monitoring during the flight. If the patient is haemodynamically unstable, ask the coordinator about stretcher fitment and whether a dedicated ICU nurse rather than a standard flight medic is required.

Before booking, prepare a concise dossier: recent PET/CT or MRI reports, histopathology report, current medication list including targeted-therapy doses, and a doctor-to-doctor referral note. The air ambulance coordinator in Dhaka can often pre-negotiate the receiving bed, so having these documents ready shortens the dispatch-to-departure window to as little as two hours.

Call 01716-960770 for immediate oncology transfer coordination. We operate 24/7 and can verify Bumrungrad or Bangkok Hospital bed availability before confirming the flight.

Continuity of Active Treatment

We can keep IV therapies going during the transfer. This includes hydration, electrolyte correction, blood transfusions, parenteral nutrition and certain chemotherapy infusions. Our infusion pumps and monitors let the flight doctor follow the plan set by your oncologist. The treatment continues without a break from Dhaka to Bangkok.

Direct Admission to Bumrungrad Cancer Centre

We plan every cancer transfer with Bumrungrad International Hospital's Cancer Centre in Bangkok. We alert their team before departure. This team includes medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, surgical oncologists, haematologists and palliative care specialists. So the patient can get a consult, imaging or treatment right after arrival.

Immunocompromised Patient Environment

For neutropenic or immunocompromised cancer patients, we can arrange a private aircraft to lower infection risk. The cabin is a controlled space with HEPA-equivalent filtration. Few people are on board, and we follow strict hygiene rules for the whole journey. This is far safer than crowded airport terminals and commercial cabins.

What Are Common Questions About Cancer Patient Air Ambulance Transfers?

What is a cancer patient air ambulance transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok?

A cancer patient air ambulance transfer is a dedicated medical flight. It is either an ICU-equipped charter jet or a commercial medical escort. Either way, it moves cancer patients safely from Dhaka to Bangkok. We tailor the service to the patient's cancer type, current treatment and stability. It comes with a trained medical team, the right monitors and equipment, and close planning with Bumrungrad International Hospital's Cancer Centre for quick admission on arrival.

How much does a cancer patient transfer cost from Dhaka to Bangkok?

The cost depends on a few things. These are the choice of ICU charter jet or commercial medical escort, the size of the medical team, the need for special equipment (such as infusion pumps, oxygen or suction), and the urgency. We give an itemised quote after we review the patient's oncology reports. Visit our air ambulance cost page for detailed pricing information.

Which Bangkok hospital treats cancer patients transferred by air ambulance?

All our cancer patients are transferred to Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok, home to a comprehensive Cancer Centre offering medical oncology, radiation oncology, surgical oncology, immunotherapy, bone marrow transplant and palliative care. Learn more on our Bangkok hospitals page.

Can chemotherapy be administered during the flight?

Yes, in many cases. During the transfer our medical team can keep IV infusions running. These include certain chemotherapy agents, hydration, antibiotics, blood products and parenteral nutrition. The flight doctor works with your oncologist so the treatment plan continues without a break. We secure all medications and infusion pumps for flight safety.

What services are included in the cancer patient transfer package?

The full service includes: ground ambulance in Dhaka and Bangkok, a dedicated aircraft or commercial medical escort, a flight doctor and nurse skilled in oncology, all monitoring and treatment equipment, permits and landing clearances, medical visa support, and direct planning with Bumrungrad's oncology team. Explore our complete services page for more details.

How does the cancer patient transfer process work?

Step 1: Call our 24/7 desk with the patient's oncology reports. Step 2: We assess the case and recommend the optimal transfer solution. Step 3: Coordinate admission with Bumrungrad Cancer Centre. Step 4: Ground ambulance collects the patient in Dhaka. Step 5: Bedside-to-ramp transfer and departure. Step 6: Fly to Bangkok with continuous oncology care. Step 7: Ground ambulance to Bumrungrad for direct handover. See our transfer process page for the full breakdown.
