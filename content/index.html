Quick Answer

Air Ambulance Service Dhaka to Bangkok is a 24/7 ICU-equipped medical flight service that transfers critically ill and injured patients bed-to-bed from Dhaka, Bangladesh to Bangkok, Thailand. Each transfer includes a qualified flight doctor, paramedic or critical-care nurse, ventilator, cardiac monitor and emergency medication, with ground ambulances coordinated at both ends. The typical Dhaka–Bangkok flight takes about 2.5–3 hours, and an air ambulance can be mobilised within hours of confirmation. Call 01716-960770 to arrange a transfer.

Our Services

What Complete Medical Transport Solutions Do We Offer?

From fully ICU-configured charter jets to medically escorted commercial flights, we match the transport mode to the patient's clinical needs and budget.

ICU Air Ambulance (Charter)

Dedicated air ambulance jet configured as a flying intensive-care unit with ventilator, defibrillator, cardiac monitor and infusion pumps for the most critical patients.

Commercial Medical Escort

Stable patients travel on scheduled flights to Bangkok accompanied by a doctor or critical-care nurse, with stretcher or seat configuration as required.

Ground Ambulance & Bed-to-Bed

Coordinated road ambulance at both Dhaka and Bangkok ends so the patient moves seamlessly from one hospital bed to the next.

Repatriation & Return Transfer

Bringing Bangladeshi patients home after treatment, or returning recovered patients from Bangkok to Dhaka under medical supervision.

Critical & Neonatal Care

Specialised transfers for cardiac, neuro, trauma, neonatal and paediatric patients with the appropriate equipment and trained crew.

Visa, Permits & Documentation

Assistance with medical visas, flight clearances, hospital appointments and the paperwork that keeps an international transfer on schedule.
