What these awards tell you

Choosing an air ambulance is a decision made under pressure, often for a critically ill loved one. These honours from Bumrungrad International Hospital — one of Asia's most respected medical institutions — show that our coordination is trusted by the hospital itself, not just by us.

  • Direct hospital partnership: Surecell Medical (BD) Ltd is a recognised referral partner of Bumrungrad International Hospital, so admissions and bed-to-bed handovers are arranged directly.
  • Proven track record: Recognition for Best Campaign, Best Brand Development and Best Performance reflects consistent, real patient coordination — not one-off cases.
  • Named, accountable team: Our Bangladesh coordination is led by professionals like Nusrat Mahbub Borney, publicly recognised for excellence in healthcare client relations.
  • End-to-end trust: From the first call in Dhaka to admission in Bangkok, families deal with an award-recognised, established organisation.
