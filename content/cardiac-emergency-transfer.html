Cardiac Emergency Air Ambulance Dhaka to Bangkok

Fast ICU air ambulance for heart attack, post-arrest, CABG and critical cardiac patients. We fly from Dhaka to Bumrungrad International Hospital Bangkok. The flight is doctor-led, fully monitored and ready 24/7.

Quick Answer

What Does a Cardiac Emergency Air Ambulance Transfer Involve?

What is a cardiac emergency air ambulance? It is a charter jet fitted as an ICU. It moves heart patients from Dhaka to Bangkok under non-stop critical care. The aircraft carries a full cardiac monitoring and life-support setup. This includes a transport ventilator, defibrillator, multi-parameter ECG monitor, infusion pumps and cardiac drugs. A flight doctor trained in advanced cardiac life support is on board. Costs vary by aircraft type and crew needs. See our air ambulance cost guide for details. Patients are treated at Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok. This service requires the full ICU air ambulance package. It takes about 3–5 hours end-to-end through our transfer process.

Cardiac Emergency Transfer

Why Choose a Cardiac Air Ambulance for Your Heart Patient?

A heart attack, unstable angina, post-CABG complication or cardiac arrest does not wait. Sometimes a patient needs more care than local facilities can give. The safest choice is then a fast transfer to a top cardiac centre. The only safe way to move an unstable heart patient is inside a flying ICU with a dedicated cardiac team.

Continuous Cardiac Monitoring at Altitude

Our air ambulance has a multi-parameter monitor. It tracks ECG rhythm, ST-segment changes, invasive blood pressure, oxygen saturation and capnography without a break. The flight doctor reads these in real time and can act at once. No commercial flight can offer this to a heart patient in distress.

Cardiac Drug & Defibrillation Capability

We can give IV inotropic support, antiarrhythmic infusions, vasopressors and sedation all through the flight. The aircraft carries a defibrillator with external pacing. For the most critical cases, it also carries intra-aortic balloon pump support. A physician trained in advanced cardiac life support (ACLS) manages all of it.

Direct Admission to Bumrungrad Heart Institute

We plan every cardiac transfer with Bumrungrad International Hospital's Heart Institute in Bangkok. We alert their 24/7 interventional cardiology team, cardiac catheterisation labs and cardiothoracic surgical unit before departure. The patient then moves from our ICU stretcher straight to their cardiac ICU with no handoff delay.

Bed-to-Bed Cardiac Transfer

From the ICU bed in Dhaka to the cardiac unit at Bumrungrad, the patient never leaves monitored care. Our ground ambulance collects the patient from the sending hospital. We then transfer them to the aircraft at the ramp. The flight runs under full monitoring. A second ground ambulance takes them on in Bangkok. Our team manages this whole chain.

People Also Ask

What Are Common Questions About Cardiac Air Ambulance Transfers?

What is a cardiac emergency air ambulance from Dhaka to Bangkok?

A cardiac emergency air ambulance is a charter jet set up as a flying intensive care unit. It is built to care for heart patients during transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok. It carries advanced cardiac monitoring, defibrillation, pacing, IV infusion pumps and a full drug box. A critical-care flight doctor with ACLS training staffs the flight. This service suits patients with acute coronary syndrome, post-cardiac arrest, worsening heart failure or post-CABG complications. It also suits those who need urgent interventional cardiology at Bumrungrad International Hospital.

How much does a cardiac air ambulance cost from Dhaka to Bangkok?

The cost depends on the aircraft type, the size of the medical crew, the equipment needed (such as IABP or nitric oxide), and how urgent the case is. We give a clear, itemised quote after we review the patient's cardiac reports. Visit our air ambulance cost page to see what drives the price of cardiac transfers.

Which Bangkok hospital treats cardiac emergency patients transferred by air ambulance?

We transfer all our cardiac emergency patients to Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok. It is home to one of Asia's leading Heart Institutes. Bumrungrad offers 24/7 interventional cardiology, cardiac surgery, electrophysiology, and a dedicated cardiac ICU. Learn more on our Bangkok hospitals page.

What cardiac equipment is on board the air ambulance?

Our cardiac air ambulance includes a transport ventilator, a 5-lead ECG/ST-segment monitor, and a defibrillator with external pacing. It also carries an intra-aortic balloon pump (on request), several infusion/syringe pumps, capnography, and invasive and non-invasive blood pressure monitoring. It has blood gas analyser capability and a full cardiac emergency drug formulary. This gear lets the flight doctor manage arrhythmias, hypotension, pulmonary oedema and pain at altitude.

What services are included in the cardiac emergency transfer package?

The full service includes a ground ambulance in Dhaka and Bangkok, a dedicated charter aircraft, and a flight doctor and critical-care paramedic. It also covers all cardiac monitoring and life-support equipment, permits and landing clearances, and medical visa support. We work directly with Bumrungrad's cardiac team. Explore our complete services page for more on what we provide.

How does the cardiac transfer process work step by step?

Step 1: Call our 24/7 desk. Step 2: We assess cardiac reports and recommend the right aircraft and crew. Step 3: We coordinate admission with Bumrungrad Heart Institute. Step 4: Ground ambulance collects the patient in Dhaka. Step 5: Bedside-to-ramp transfer, aircraft departure under cardiac monitoring. Step 6: Fly to Bangkok with continuous ACLS-level care. Step 7: Ground ambulance to Bumrungrad, direct handover to the cardiac ICU team. See our transfer process page for the full breakdown.
