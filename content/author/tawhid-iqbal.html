About Tawhid

Tawhid Iqbal is an air ambulance and medical-travel coordinator based in Dhaka. He works hands-on with Bangladeshi families who need to move a critically ill or injured relative from Dhaka to Bangkok for specialist treatment — arranging ICU-equipped charter flights, commercial medical escorts and bed-to-bed transfers to Bumrungrad International and other Bangkok hospitals.

His writing on this site comes from real coordination experience: handling flight permits, aircraft selection, medical crews, ground ambulances at both ends, hospital admissions and insurance paperwork. The goal of every guide is simple — give families honest, practical information so they can make calm decisions during an emergency.

Credentials & Professional Background

Tawhid holds a diploma in Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) training completed at the Bangladesh Institute of Health Sciences, followed by advanced certification in aeromedical transport coordination through a regional aviation medicine programme. This paramedic-level clinical foundation gives him the practical language to work directly with flight doctors, ICU nurses and critical care paramedics — and to accurately assess whether a patient's condition requires a full ICU charter jet or a monitored commercial medical escort.

Over more than eight years working on the Dhaka–Bangkok corridor, he has built direct working relationships with the international patient departments at Bumrungrad International Hospital, BNH Hospital and Samitivej Hospital in Bangkok, as well as with aeromedical charter operators, ground ambulance services and medical flight permit authorities in Bangladesh and Thailand. He is fluent in Bengali, English and conversational Thai medical terminology. He is also an active member of the Air Medical Operators Association (AMOA) regional network, through which he stays current on aeromedical safety standards, equipment certifications and regulatory changes affecting cross-border medical transport in South and Southeast Asia.

Experience & background

Tawhid has spent more than eight years coordinating cross-border medical transfers from Bangladesh. In that time he has helped arrange over 500 safe patient movements — from ventilator-dependent ICU cases to stable medical-escort flights — most of them on the Dhaka to Bangkok route. He works daily with aeromedical crews, charter operators, ground-ambulance teams and the international patient desks at Bumrungrad, Samitivej and Bangkok Hospital.

That hands-on work shapes everything published here. He has managed cardiac and stroke emergencies where minutes mattered, neonatal incubator transfers, post-accident trauma evacuations and end-of-life repatriations home to Bangladesh. Each guide reflects situations he has handled in person, not theory.

Notable Case Experience & Expertise Areas

Among the most complex transfers Tawhid has coordinated are ventilator-dependent STEMI patients requiring primary PCI at Bumrungrad's Heart Institute within narrow treatment windows, post-cardiac arrest patients on targeted temperature management during the flight, neonates in transport incubators with congenital heart defects transferred to Samitivej Children's Hospital's NICU, and haemorrhagic stroke patients requiring emergent neurosurgical intervention coordinated with Bumrungrad's neurosurgery team. He has also managed several dozen oncology transfers — from patients requiring urgent chemotherapy to those seeking second opinions on complex cancer diagnoses — and multiple post-operative repatriations returning patients home to Bangladesh following successful treatment in Bangkok.

His specialist expertise includes: pre-flight fitness-to-fly assessment and coordination with sending ICU physicians; aircraft selection matched to patient acuity and mission profile; permit and documentation management including Thai medical visas, Bangladeshi civil aviation overflight clearances, and customs documentation for medical equipment; insurance pre-authorisation and claims support; and post-transfer follow-up to ensure continuity of care at the receiving Bangkok hospital.
