About Tawhid

Tawhid Iqbal is an air ambulance and medical-travel coordinator based in Dhaka. He works hands-on with Bangladeshi families who need to move a critically ill or injured relative from Dhaka to Bangkok for specialist treatment — arranging ICU-equipped charter flights, commercial medical escorts and bed-to-bed transfers to Bumrungrad International and other Bangkok hospitals.

His writing on this site comes from real coordination experience: handling flight permits, aircraft selection, medical crews, ground ambulances at both ends, hospital admissions and insurance paperwork. The goal of every guide is simple — give families honest, practical information so they can make calm decisions during an emergency.

Experience & background

Tawhid has spent more than eight years coordinating cross-border medical transfers from Bangladesh. In that time he has helped arrange over 500 safe patient movements — from ventilator-dependent ICU cases to stable medical-escort flights — most of them on the Dhaka to Bangkok route. He works daily with aeromedical crews, charter operators, ground-ambulance teams and the international patient desks at Bumrungrad, Samitivej and Bangkok Hospital.

That hands-on work shapes everything published here. He has managed cardiac and stroke emergencies where minutes mattered, neonatal incubator transfers, post-accident trauma evacuations and end-of-life repatriations home to Bangladesh. Each guide reflects situations he has handled in person, not theory.

How our guides are written & reviewed

Every article is written from operational experience and reviewed by our in-house medical coordination team for clinical accuracy before publication. Articles show their published and reviewed dates, and we update them as procedures, pricing and hospital details change.

The information on this site is general guidance about medical transport — it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
