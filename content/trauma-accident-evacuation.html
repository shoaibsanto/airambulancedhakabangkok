Trauma & Accident Emergency Air Ambulance Dhaka to Bangkok

Rapid-response ICU air ambulance for polytrauma, head injury, spinal cord injury and accident victims from Dhaka to Bumrungrad International Hospital Bangkok — ATLS-led, fully equipped and available 24/7.

Quick Answer

What Does a Trauma Air Ambulance Transfer from Dhaka to Bangkok Involve?

What is a trauma and accident emergency air ambulance? A dedicated ICU charter jet that rapidly transfers trauma victims from Dhaka to Bangkok under continuous critical care. The aircraft carries ATLS-level equipment — ventilator, defibrillator, chest drain kit, spinal immobilisation, massive haemorrhage protocol supplies and blood products — staffed by a doctor trained in advanced trauma life support. Costs depend on aircraft, crew and equipment needs — see our air ambulance cost guide. Patients are treated at Bumrungrad International Hospital's Trauma Centre in Bangkok. This service requires the full ICU air ambulance package and takes approximately 3–5 hours end-to-end via our coordinated transfer process.

Trauma Evacuation

Why Does a Dedicated Trauma Air Ambulance Matter for Accident Victims?

Road traffic accident by accident insurance coverages, industrial injuries, falls and violent trauma can produce injury patterns that overwhelm local surgical capacity. For Bangladeshi patients with polytrauma, traumatic brain injury, spinal cord injury, crush injuries or severe burns, the difference between survival and permanent disability often depends on how fast they reach a fully equipped trauma centre — and the quality of care they receive during the transfer.

ATLS-Trained Flight Doctor

Every trauma transfer is led by a flight doctor trained in Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS). They can perform needle decompression, chest drain insertion, cricothyroidotomy, fracture splinting, pelvic binding, haemorrhage control and massive transfusion en route — interventions that can be life-saving during the golden hour window between Dhaka and Bangkok.

Full Trauma Resuscitation Capability

Our aircraft carries a comprehensive trauma kit: spinal immobilisation board, cervical collars, pelvic binder, lower-limb traction splints, chest drainage system, massive haemorrhage protocol supplies including packed red blood cells or whole blood (when indicated), warming blanket, and a full trauma drug box including tranexamic acid, calcium, vasopressors and anaesthetic agents.

Direct Admission to Bumrungrad Trauma Centre

We coordinate every trauma transfer with Bumrungrad International Hospital's 24/7 Trauma Centre in Bangkok. Their in-house orthopaedic, neurosurgical, cardiothoracic, plastic and general surgery teams are pre-alerted, and the CT scanner, operating theatre and ICU are prepared before the patient lands — enabling immediate surgical intervention.

Bed-to-Bed Trauma Transfer

From the accident site or hospital in Dhaka to the trauma unit at Bumrungrad, the patient remains immobilised, monitored and stabilised throughout. Ground ambulance collection, rampside transfer, ICU flight and final road leg — all managed by our team with continuous clinical oversight and zero handoff gaps.

People Also Ask

What Are Common Questions About Trauma Air Ambulance Transfers?

What is a trauma accident emergency air ambulance from Dhaka to Bangkok?

A trauma accident emergency air ambulance is a dedicated ICU charter jet configured for the rapid evacuation of polytrauma, head injury, spinal injury, crush injury, burn and other accident victims from Dhaka to Bangkok. The aircraft carries ATLS-level resuscitation equipment, spinal immobilisation, massive haemorrhage protocol supplies and blood product capability. The flight doctor is trained in advanced trauma life support and can perform critical procedures en route. This service is designed for patients who require urgent surgical or intensive care at Bumrungrad International Hospital's Trauma Centre.

How much does a trauma evacuation cost from Dhaka to Bangkok?

The cost varies based on aircraft type, medical crew size, equipment requirements (such as blood products or specific surgical supplies), and the urgency of the evacuation. We provide transparent, itemised quotes after reviewing the patient's trauma assessment. Visit our air ambulance cost page for a detailed breakdown of pricing factors for trauma transfers.

Which Bangkok hospital treats trauma emergency patients transferred by air ambulance?

All our trauma and accident emergency patients are transferred to Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok, which operates a comprehensive 24/7 trauma centre with multi-specialty surgical teams, advanced imaging and dedicated trauma ICUs. Learn more on our Bangkok hospitals page.

What trauma equipment is on board the air ambulance?

Our trauma air ambulance includes: transport ventilator, multi-parameter monitor, defibrillator, infusion/syringe pumps, spinal immobilisation board and collars, pelvic binder, traction splints, chest drainage system, massive haemorrhage protocol supplies including blood products, warming blanket, suction, and a full trauma drug formulary including TXA, calcium, vasopressors and anaesthetics.

What services are included in the trauma evacuation package?

The full service includes: ground ambulance in Dhaka and Bangkok, dedicated charter aircraft, ATLS-trained flight doctor and paramedic, all trauma resuscitation equipment, blood products if indicated, permits and landing clearances, medical visa support, and direct coordination with Bumrungrad's trauma and surgical teams. Explore our complete services page for more details.

How does the trauma evacuation process work step by step?

Step 1: Call our 24/7 desk. Step 2: Trauma triage and aircraft recommendation. Step 3: Coordinate admission with Bumrungrad Trauma Centre. Step 4: Ground ambulance collects patient in Dhaka. Step 5: Bedside-to-ramp transfer with spinal immobilisation if needed. Step 6: Fly to Bangkok with ATLS-level care. Step 7: Ground ambulance to Bumrungrad trauma unit. See our transfer process page for the full breakdown.
