Why Bumrungrad International Hospital Is Our Primary Bangkok Partner

Bumrungrad International Hospital

A JCI-accredited hospital known across Asia for complex cardiac surgery and interventional cardiology, oncology and cancer care, and neurology and neurosurgery. Bangladeshi patients are often referred here for high-acuity sub-specialty treatment and its well-developed international patient services, which make admission for a transferred patient straightforward.

We do not hold any official affiliation or partnership with Bumrungrad International Hospital — our role is to coordinate the air ambulance leg and the clinical handover. If you already know you want Bumrungrad, we will plan the transfer to it. You can review the full Dhaka to Bangkok flight route and ground transfer for how the journey is structured.

Why Bumrungrad International Hospital?

Bumrungrad handles the highest number of Bangladeshi patients of any hospital in Bangkok and has a dedicated international admissions office. It is the strongest choice for interventional cardiology, complex cardiac surgery, oncology (including bone marrow transplant), and neurology. Its Joint Commission International (JCI) accreditation means the hospital meets global standards for patient safety and clinical governance — an important consideration when admitting a patient who has already been stabilised in Dhaka and needs continued high-acuity care.

If you know Bumrungrad is the right facility, we coordinate directly with their international admissions team. If you are unsure, we can help you evaluate based on the patient's diagnosis and clinical requirements. Coordination

How Hospital Coordination & Bed-to-Bed Handover Works

Before the aircraft departs Dhaka, we contact Bumrungrad International Hospital in Bangkok and confirm that they can take the patient. This means checking bed or ICU availability, confirming the relevant specialist or department is ready, and aligning the planned arrival time with the hospital's admission process. A transfer is only finalised once the destination has confirmed it can receive the patient — we never fly a critical patient toward an unconfirmed bed.

We then share the clinical handover with the receiving medical team: the patient's diagnosis, current status, medications, ventilator and oxygen requirements, and recent investigations. This lets the Bangkok specialists prepare before arrival and continue care without gaps. Our medical escort carries the documentation and equipment needed to keep the same standard of monitoring in the air as on the ground.

On the Thailand side, we arrange the ground ambulance from Suvarnabhumi Airport to Bumrungrad, synchronised with the flight so there is no waiting on the apron. The patient moves directly from the aircraft to an equipped ground ambulance and on to the confirmed hospital bed, completing a true bed-to-bed handover. Why It Matters

Why Does the Receiving Hospital Matter for Medical Transfers?

Choosing the right destination hospital is part of a safe transfer, not an afterthought. A patient who needs interventional cardiology, neurosurgery or specialist oncology should arrive somewhere equipped to continue that exact care the moment the doors open — otherwise the journey simply adds risk and delay. Matching the patient's condition to a hospital with the right sub-specialty, the right level of critical care and an admission team ready to receive them is what turns a long flight into a genuine continuation of treatment.

Because we coordinate the transfer directly with Bumrungrad's receiving team, the hospital is expecting the patient, the bed is held, and the handover is clinical rather than administrative. If you would like to discuss whether Bumrungrad best fits the patient's needs, learn about our air ambulance service from Dhaka to Bangkok or arrange a transfer to Bumrungrad International Hospital with our flight desk.
