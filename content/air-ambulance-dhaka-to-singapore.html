Air Ambulance Dhaka to Singapore — ICU Jet 24/7

Fully equipped ICU jet transfers from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (Dhaka) to National University Hospital, Mount Elizabeth, or Singapore General Hospital. Flight time 4 to 4.5 hours. Call 01716-960770.

Quick Answer

Air ambulance from Dhaka to Singapore costs between USD 32,000 and USD 42,000 (BDT 38,00,000 to 50,00,000) for a fully equipped ICU charter. The Learjet 35A or King Air 350 flies directly from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport to Singapore Changi in 4 to 4.5 hours, with a flight doctor and paramedic providing continuous ICU care throughout.

What Does an Air Ambulance from Dhaka to Singapore Cost?

The cost of an air ambulance from Dhaka to Singapore ranges from USD 32,000 to USD 42,000 (approximately BDT 38,00,000 to BDT 50,00,000). This is an all-inclusive charter price for a fully equipped flying ICU using a Learjet 35A or Beechcraft King Air 350, departing from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport. The higher cost compared to Bangkok reflects the additional 900 km of flight distance and the 4 to 4.5 hour flight time versus 3.5 hours to Bangkok.

Which Singapore Hospitals Accept Air Ambulance Patients?

Singapore has five globally ranked hospitals that regularly receive critically ill patients from Bangladesh via air ambulance. We maintain pre-arranged admission agreements and direct specialist contacts at each:

  • National University Hospital (NUH) — Singapore's largest public teaching hospital; strong in oncology, neurosurgery, organ transplant, and rare conditions
  • Singapore General Hospital (SGH) — Excellent for cardiac surgery, burns, trauma, and multi-organ failure; Level 1 trauma centre
  • Mount Elizabeth Hospital (Parkway) — Premium private facility; common for elective cardiac, orthopaedic, and cancer procedures
  • Tan Tock Seng Hospital (TTSH) — Renowned infectious disease centre and strong in rehabilitation medicine
  • KK Women's and Children's Hospital (KKH) — Singapore's primary paediatric and neonatal intensive care centre

When you call us, our medical coordination team reviews the patient's diagnosis, treatment summary, and insurance documents to recommend the most appropriate hospital — and confirms bed availability before the aircraft is dispatched.

When Should You Choose Singapore Over Bangkok?

Bangkok's Bumrungrad International Hospital and Samitivej Hospital offer comparable specialist care at a lower total transfer cost ($27,000–$33,000). However, Singapore is the preferred destination for:

  • Organ transplant — Singapore hospitals have active living-donor transplant programmes
  • Advanced oncology — When specialist immunotherapy or rare cancer protocols are needed
  • Paediatric ICU cases — Neonatal and paediatric patients needing subspecialist care at KKH
  • Burns patients — Singapore General Hospital has one of the region's best burns units
  • Rare neurological conditions — Complex epilepsy, neuromuscular disease, brain tumour surgery

For time-critical emergencies (cardiac, stroke, trauma), Bangkok remains the better destination because the flight is 1 hour shorter. See our complete Dhaka to Singapore air ambulance guide for a detailed comparison.

What Is the Air Ambulance Process from Dhaka to Singapore?

  1. Call 01716-960770 — Our duty doctor reviews the patient's medical records and confirms whether Singapore or another destination is optimal.
  2. Aircraft mobilisation — We dispatch the appropriate aircraft from its standby position. Aircraft positioning to Dhaka typically takes 1–2 hours.
  3. Export medical clearance — Our operations team submits the medical export documentation to Bangladesh authorities simultaneously.
  4. Receiving hospital confirmation — We confirm bed availability, specialist team briefing, and ICU or ward assignment in Singapore before the patient is moved.
  5. Ground ambulance — Dhaka hospital to airport — Fully equipped critical care ground ambulance with the flight medical team aboard.
  6. Flight (4–4.5 hours) — Uninterrupted ICU care en route. Ventilated patients remain on the transport ventilator throughout.
  7. Arrival Singapore Changi + handover — Ground ambulance to receiving hospital. Medical handover directly to the receiving specialist team.

Can a Ventilated Patient Travel to Singapore?

Yes. Ventilated patients are a routine transfer for us. The Learjet 35A and King Air 350 are configured to carry stretcher patients on full mechanical ventilation for the entire 4 to 4.5 hour flight. The flight doctor monitors and adjusts ventilator settings continuously en route. Singapore hospitals are pre-briefed to receive ventilated patients directly to ICU.

Do I Need a Singapore Visa for Medical Treatment?

Bangladesh passport holders require a visa to enter Singapore. For medical emergencies, Singapore's Ministry of Health has an expedited medical visa process. Our coordination team assists with the visa application letter from the receiving hospital. In genuine emergencies, the Singapore High Commission in Dhaka has issued same-day medical visas with our support documentation.

How Does Insurance Work for Air Ambulance to Singapore?

Most international health insurance policies covering Bangladesh residents include medical evacuation benefits. We work directly with insurance companies to obtain pre-authorisation before the flight departs — the family does not need to pay out of pocket in most cases where insurance applies. See our insurance coverage guide for details.
